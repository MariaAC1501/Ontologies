# OntoCast development runs

These directories are lightweight records of historical extraction experiments. They are retained to explain development decisions about corpus selection, coverage, retries, model benchmarking, and subscription usage.

Retained material includes:

- run, resume, and coverage manifests;
- selection and coverage reports, selected-paper lists, and small source-provenance lists;
- the benchmark report, summary, metrics, and blind-judge data; and
- the subscription usage report.

Raw inputs and source documents are not duplicated here. Generated facts and ontology TTL files, normal and pre-quota output snapshots, retry outputs, logs, PID/lock and other process-control files, and temporary products are intentionally excluded because they are reproducible runtime artifacts. The local `.gitignore` enforces this boundary without ignoring the retained manifests and reports.

These runs are exploratory development history only. **None of this material is publication evidence**, and it must not be cited or used as support for publication claims.
